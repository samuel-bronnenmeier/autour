# AuTour

[![de](https://img.shields.io/badge/lang-de-red.svg)](https://github.com/samuel-bronnenmeier/autour/blob/master/README.de.md)

## Version 1

This is a an attempt at simplifying the process of taking on tours as a tour guide, quite specifically for Zoo Augsburg, but practically for any tour guide system with asimilar structure. It lets you input the tours you want to do, automatically generates an e-mail with all important information to your boss (currently: copy the generated e-mail, paste it in your mail-program, send manually, plan: send automatically), and then when your boss answers, which tours are actually yours, it offers the possibility to load in all the tours you inputted, delete the ones you didn't get and send you an e-mail with an iCal-file, which lets you easily import it in Apple Calendar, Google Calendar or similar.

## Version 2 (in progress)

Lets you import the pdfs directly once downloaded from the e-mail from your boss as a zip, so you can simply click on the tours you want to take. Automatically sends an e-mail to your boss and reads the answer to determine which tours you actually got to send you and e-mail with an iCal-file to import in your calendar app of choice.

## Installation

1. Download the exe into a folder of your choice.
2. Execute the exe and dismiss warnings. The app will automatically install into default location and genereate a desktop icon for quick access.

## Workflow

1. Input all the tours you want to take.
2. Let the app generate the e-mail to your boss automatically.
3. Copy the suggested e-mail, paste it in your mail-program and change it to fit your needs.
4. Wait for an answer.
5. Open the app again and load in the data of your last session (the tours you last inputted and generated an e-mail for).
6. Delete the ones you didn't get.
7. Export the tours as an iCal-file. This will send an e-mail to yourself with an attachment: Open it (by clicking on it) to impott all the tours into your calnedar.
